# Player

One focal point: the cover or the video. Five primary controls (shuffle, previous, play, next, repeat). The heart sits by the title. Queue and lyrics are the right-hand tabs, not extra icons. Sparkle, the view menu (cinema, fullscreen, mini) and settings are three quiet icons on the volume line.

Spacing stays generous. Type is two sizes: the title, and everything else. Color is the dark surface (`#101010`, `#181818`, `#f3f3f1`) with one accent taken from the artwork when the fullscreen lyric stage is open. Motion is a short opacity change; reduced-motion turns it off.

Taken from Apple Music Now Playing and its fullscreen lyrics, Spotify’s desktop Now Playing, and the quieter layouts of Tidal, Plexamp and Marvis: a large picture, a short control row, and the rest behind a menu. The phone uses the same colors and the same five controls, with a bottom bar and a full-screen now playing sheet.
